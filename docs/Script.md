## Slide 2:

Everyone has a need to be able to issue and verify records to anyone and from anyone without any sort of centralized gatekeeping organization getting in the way.  

Nostr Safebox, in addition to payments, is intended to embody this capability through private and secure record sharing that leverages the web of trust concepts

I hope to present a working solution that works end-to-end that is ready for pilot. Today's presentation is geared towards the sharing of health records.

I took my inspiration from the simple nosfabrica vision - deliver care and receive payment. In the previous hackation, I have demonstrated payment, so now I will now focus on demonstrating delivery of care through record sharing.

## Slide 3:

Here is a mock-up ad. The patient does not have a phone, but rather a NFC bracelet that is a nostr safebox. I will demonstrate how care can be delivered without a phone! I am also focusing on making safebox as inclusive as possible so it will work with legacy phones.
