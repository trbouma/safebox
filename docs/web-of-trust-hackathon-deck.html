Safebox: Web of Trust Record Verification

Issued, Held, Presented, Verified

A Nostr-native wallet and records system with staged trust verification.

Speaker Note

Introduce Safebox as a Nostr-native wallet and records system and frame the talk around a simple issued-to-holder verification flow.

  • Safebox is intended to be client-zero.
  • A playful variation on “patient-zero”.
  • An early real client demonstrating the practical potential of this group’s Web of Trust work.
  • Grounded especially in NIP-85 Trusted Assertions and Attestations.

Identity Neutrality

npub is the base unit of accountable identity.

  • No protocol distinction between person, agent, service, device, or Safebox instance.
  • Issuer, holder, presenter, and verifier are functional roles layered on the same identity primitive.
  • Trust and policy are added above identity, not encoded into identity class.

Speaker Note

Explain that Safebox and Nostr do not hard-code identity classes.

  • If these NIPs are going to matter, they need to be exercised by a real client.
  • Safebox is trying to be that proving ground for live Web of Trust flows.

Speaker Note

Safebox does not declare ultimate truth. It gives the verifier enough structured evidence to decide when enough checks have passed to act.

  • Attestations support ownership and issuer assertions.
  • NIP-85 supports trusted advisory signals.
  • Safebox combines them into a verifier-facing acceptance flow.

What You’re About To See
